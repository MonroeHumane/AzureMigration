About charitable remainder trusts
A charitable remainder trust may work well if you want to donate appreciated assets, receive an income tax deduction, and reserve income for yourself or loved ones. Ask your advisor whether a CRT is appropriate for your situation.

Helpful details

Memorial gift questions

Will the family know a gift was made?
Yes. We send acknowledgment letters to the family and the donor. The gift amount remains private.
When will a tribute appear on the honor roll?
Staff reviews each memorial gift after its details are received and confirmed. If a tribute is time-sensitive or does not appear as expected, please contact us.
How do I request a correction?
Email jackie@monroe-humane.org with the name as it currently appears and the requested correction.
Can my gift be anonymous?
Yes. Tell us on the memorial gift form—or contact us directly—if you do not want your name shared with the family or listed publicly.
Are donation amounts shown publicly?
No. The public honor roll recognizes the person or pet being honored; donation amounts are not displayed.
