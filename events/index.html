Add to Your Calendar

Subscribe once and new shelter fundraisers, adoption promotions, and charity clinic dates will automatically appear on your phone or computer.

Calendar Feed URL:
iPhone / Mac (Apple Calendar):

Tap the green "Sync (.ics)" button, or in Calendar choose File > New Calendar Subscription and paste the feed URL.

Google Calendar:

Open Google Calendar on desktop, click "+" next to "Other calendars", select "From URL", and paste the feed URL.

Outlook / Office 365:

In Outlook Calendar, choose "Add calendar" > "Subscribe from web", paste the feed URL, and click Import.
