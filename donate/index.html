Donate to Monroe Humane

The Humane Society of Monroe County is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit receiving zero tax funding. Every dollar stays right here in Monroe County, providing medical surgery, wholesome food, and safe refuge until every pet finds a loving home.

100% to Monroe County Animals • 0% Fees Deducted

Through our nonprofit partnership with Zeffy, 100% of your gift goes directly to veterinary medicine, nourishing food, and daily shelter care. Zero platform or credit card processing fees are deducted.
