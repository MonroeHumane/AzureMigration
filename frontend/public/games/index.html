About Humane Games

Small games for big hearts

Humane Games is a tiny browser arcade made for Monroe Humane supporters. Everything loads fast, plays nicely on older devices, and stays gentle, no ads, no trackers, no surprise sound.

Grab a quick mood boost, then hop back to your day or visit the shelter to see how you can help.
