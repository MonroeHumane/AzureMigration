Membership FAQs

Quick answers about member fulfillment, store discounts, and giving.

When does 2026 membership enrollment open?↓

Official 2026 enrollment is opening soon! In the meantime, you can review the tiers and perks above, or support shelter operations today by making a tax-deductible general donation.

How do I receive my member shirt and perks?↓

Member welcome kits (membership card, winning t-shirt, bumper sticker, and keychain) can be picked up at the shelter during open hours or shipped to your address. Select your preference and shirt size (Unisex XS–4XL) during checkout.

How does the HSMC Shelter Store discount work?↓

Beagle Buddies (25% off) and Spaniel Squad / Saint Bernard Society (50% off) members enjoy store discounts at our shelter boutique at 911 S. Raisinville Rd. Simply show your 2026 membership card at checkout.

Can I give monthly instead of annual?↓

Yes! When enrollment opens, you can choose monthly recurring giving for Beagle Buddies ($10/mo), Spaniel Squad ($20/mo), or Saint Bernard Society ($40/mo). Kitty Circle is an annual tier ($60/yr).

Is my membership contribution tax-deductible?↓

Yes. The Humane Society of Monroe County is an independent 501(c)(3) non-profit (EIN: 38-1447274). Your gift is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Tax receipts are issued immediately.

Questions About Membership or Sponsorship?

Call us at (734) 243-3669 or visit us at 911 S. Raisinville Rd, Monroe, MI 48161.
